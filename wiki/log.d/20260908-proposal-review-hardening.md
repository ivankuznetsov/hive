Proposal admission now intersects canonical-shaped evidence with configured
classification policy, revalidates evaluator authority for new events, reserves
terminal capacity, and applies namespace-wide quotas. Recovery verifies durable
terminal commits, retains transient failures, bounds lock acquisition and index
growth, and exposes terminal source quarantine through live and compiled views.

Automatic prompt context contains only closed identifiers and enums and retains
a ranked whole-item prefix. The deterministic compiler renders safe evidence and
provenance references, treats pinned symlinks as inert input, and the managed wiki
hook selects queued sources by ancestry while protecting generated proposal files
in every refresh.
