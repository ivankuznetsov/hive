---
title: Pin CI Linux runner to Ubuntu 24.04
date: 2026-10-26
---

**Action:** Pinned every Linux job in `.github/workflows/ci.yml` to
`ubuntu-24.04`. GitHub's `ubuntu-latest` migration to Ubuntu 26.04 made
Playwright 1.60's Chromium installer unsupported and caused the project
Bubblewrap sandbox tests to fail before application behavior was exercised.
