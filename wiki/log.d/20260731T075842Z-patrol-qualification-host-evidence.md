## Patrol qualification now reconstructs ordinary evidence on the host

The compressed Patrol harness now exercises all five ordinary fault boundaries,
the real one-hour retry path, concurrent duplicate delivery, and reconciliation
failure. After each candidate case, the lane controller reconstructs its event,
decisions, attempt lineage, capture, effect receipts, comparator row, retirement
fence, and repository identity from raw stores and retains that projection as a
per-case artifact. Candidate-reported restart, checkpoint, trace, and timed
state-digest claims remain explicitly unverified pending externally supervised
multi-generation execution.

Both candidate lanes are networkless and secretless. Installed-lane blocked
checks are append-only retryable diagnostics, while required E2E coverage now
demands independent protected-main control plus passed deterministic and
installed lanes. Architecture Patrol clean-negative and positive lifecycle
controls are implemented but are not yet the complete qualifying matrix.
