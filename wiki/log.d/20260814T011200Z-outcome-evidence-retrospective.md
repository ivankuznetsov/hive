- Resolved isolated provider commands past tool-manager launchers without
  inheriting opaque session variables, while retaining the closed child
  environment used by inference, production, and review roles.
- Made producer representation paths explicitly task-relative and rooted under
  the controller-issued attempt directory, then removed raw producer work after
  controller custody admission or failure.
- Embedded the exact minimal proof-entry and media-type contract in the producer
  prompt and required real product behavior; generated slides, diagrams,
  terminal-styled composites, narrated summaries, and pictures of test output
  are explicitly non-evidence for behavior claims.
- Fixed role receipts when a provider uses its default model route rather than
  an explicit routing receipt.
- Retrospectively evaluated the 159-path conditional-plan-critique task. The new
  system inferred four outcome-specific proof requirements and two justified
  exclusions, accepted the exclusions, rejected three rounds of synthetic or
  commandless behavior substitutes, and blocked after the bounded recaptures.
  The historical zero-path Hivebox capture remains diagnostic-only.
