Plan and execute now consume accepted, ordered guidance bindings and display the
pinned execution-plan version and digest in their prompts. Each launch verifies
the runtime and installed skill fingerprint and matches the exact actor and
envelope. Native defaults used for matching are pinned before launch. Missing,
changed or specialist-only bindings block explicitly. Unsupported stage runners
cannot silently discard selected guidance. Feature adoption remains disabled by
default pending verification, gates and recovery delivery.
