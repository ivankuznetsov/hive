---
title: Reconcile review decision claims before operator escalation
date: 2026-09-13
---

Plan review now reassesses open gated/manual findings against existing
authorization, consolidates duplicate concerns with complete source accounting,
and records immutable triage attempts and application receipts. Existing
unassessed awaiting-decision reviews become runnable. Routine planner revisions
and independent verification can proceed while unrelated operator choices stay
explicitly unresolved; execution clearance and revision caps remain enforced.
New verifier gates are reconciled with existing unanswered gates, preventing
a recurring concern from becoming a second decision on a later pass.
See [[modules/plan_review]].
