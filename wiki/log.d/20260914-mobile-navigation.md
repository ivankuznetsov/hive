---
title: Collapse phone navigation behind a hamburger
---

At widths up to 720px, the primary navigation and account controls now open
from a labelled hamburger button. The menu reports its expanded state, supports
Escape, and closes on navigation and before Turbo caching. Desktop keeps the
inline links. Browser coverage exercises opening, dismissal, navigation,
active links, containment, and resizing back to desktop.

Mobile Screenote feedback also restores the existing yellow-on-black Hive mark
in the header and removes the project-add link's desktop top margin on phones
so the two project controls align.
