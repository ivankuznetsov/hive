title: Repository-aware brainstorm answer suggestions
tags: [brainstorm, suggestions, daemon, tui, web, sandbox]
---

- Publish normal brainstorm questions before asynchronously reconciling one
  advisory suggestion for every unanswered physical slot.
- Bind candidates to bounded tracked-only repository/wiki evidence and settled
  operator answers; keep raw context and rejected output out of the canonical
  owner-private sidecar.
- Launch only the Claude profile's enforceable data-only Bubblewrap route,
  validate safe plain text and controller-derived provenance, and fail closed
  for unsupported isolation or stale inputs.
- Project current candidates through inert TUI envelopes and reversible Web
  controls while preserving the existing answer writer as the sole authority.
- Cancel and remove actionable state on answers/stage exit, and require the
  idempotent `hive brainstorm-suggestion cleanup --json` receipt before disable,
  revert, or downgrade.
