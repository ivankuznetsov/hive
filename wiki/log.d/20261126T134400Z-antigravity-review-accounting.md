## Antigravity review accounting

Hive retains the model reported by the Antigravity conversation and the token
counts reported by the run. Plan-review attempts persist the metered values in
their route receipts. The usage ledger records the requested and observed
models separately. Missing conversation identity keeps independence unverified,
and Hive omits malformed usage from the receipt. See [[modules/agent_profile]],
[[modules/plan_review]] and [[token-usage]].
