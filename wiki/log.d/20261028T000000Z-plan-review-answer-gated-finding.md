## [2026-10-28] fix | Plan review: answer a gated finding with a chosen alternative

**Action:** `hive plan-review TARGET answer-finding` now accepts open `gated_auto` findings as well as manual ones. Decision triage gives every gated finding a list of operator alternatives, but `approve-finding` (take the reviewer's recommendation) was the only action that could clear one. An operator who chose a listed alternative, such as hivedev C4 keeping a restore hold for its owning sibling task instead of the reviewer's self-classification, got `finding is not an open manual finding` and the review stayed parked. The answered finding records the operator's answer. Verification reopens it as `answered`, not `approved`, and the planner revision prompt now follows an answered gated finding's `answer` exactly, even where it declines the recommendation. Updated [[modules/plan_review]]. Tests: `test/unit/plan_review/decision_service_test.rb`, `test/unit/plan_review/orchestrator_test.rb`.
