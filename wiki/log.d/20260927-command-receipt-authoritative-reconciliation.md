title: Command receipts bind retirement to authoritative results
---

- Keyed command boundaries persist their exact replay envelope as effect
  evidence before the terminal receipt commit. A matching retry can finish a
  lost finalization without repeating the command body.
- Successful evidence retirement now requires that stored result and exact
  observations recorded by task-activity or GitHub publication authorities;
  caller-created results and observation labels cannot establish success.
- Interrupted operations resume only when their stored submissions belong to a
  bounded reconciler with a released owner. Unknown effects remain unresolved.
